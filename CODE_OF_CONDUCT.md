# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GroundedPDF a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for
the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that
are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for
behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report abusive behavior privately to the repository owner or through the hosting platform's maintainer
contact. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Scope and attribution

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project. It is
adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
